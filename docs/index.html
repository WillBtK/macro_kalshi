Macro Markets — Kalshi-implied forecasts

Market-implied forecast distributions extracted from Kalshi macro contracts, replicating the method of Diercks, Katz & Wright (2026), “Kalshi and the Rise of Macro Markets” (NBER WP 34702). Each thin line is the market-implied path (the forecast term structure) as priced on a past date; the bold line is what actually happened.

Data: Kalshi trades → distributions/moments (this pipeline) + realized outcomes from FRED. Original method: Diercks, Katz & Wright (2026).

Connecting to the data store…

Reads live from Supabase (PostgREST) with a public read-only key; realized underlyings from FRED via the daily pipeline. Forward curves plot each contract’s market-implied mean at its target date, as priced on the labelled as-of date (nearest observation within ~6 weeks). Contract target dates are the nominal period mid-month.
